# Privacy Policy

**TalkNative** ("the app") is built to keep your data on your device.

## What we collect

TalkNative has no accounts, no analytics, and no telemetry. We do not collect, transmit, or have access to any of your data.

## On-device processing (default)

On iPhones with Apple Intelligence, all text you enhance is processed entirely on your device using Apple's Foundation Models. It never leaves your phone.

## Cloud fallback (optional, consent-based)

On iPhones without Apple Intelligence, TalkNative offers an optional cloud fallback tier. It is off by default. If you turn it on in Settings, only the text you choose to enhance is sent over HTTPS to the TalkNative gateway, which forwards it to Anthropic's Claude model to generate the rewrite. No other data is sent, and no data is stored server-side beyond what's needed to serve that single request. You can turn this off at any time in Settings.

## Local storage

Your recent enhancement history (last 50 items) and any custom presets you create are stored locally on your device only. They are never synced or uploaded, and you can clear them at any time from Settings → History.

## Third parties

We do not sell or share your data with third parties. The only external service TalkNative ever contacts is the TalkNative gateway described above, and only when you've explicitly enabled cloud fallback mode.
